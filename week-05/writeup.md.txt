## What I Built

I built an AI team (Supply analyst, Supply writer, Customer writer, and reviewer) using two coordinators that draft email responses to suppliers. If a supplier writes an email about a purchase order, the AI team works out which customer orders are affected and drafts replies which is checked before it is sent. I tested it on Copperline Coffee Machines by making changes such as adding a --org option, using generic names, etc.

## Why This Approach

Task A: One call because the AI is able to read emails, look up the needed details, and draft a reply. An agent can assist with this as well but would cost more therefore One call would be a better option. 

Task B: Team because this is a bigger task which may require multiple agents to take on different roles such as finding machines, draft notices, draft reports, and making deciding coordinators fit for the tasks based on given information. This would be too much for one agent to handle. 

Task C: Script because it is the same steps therefore it can follow commands that was written for it. It would be a waste of money to use AI or an agent. 

Task D: Team because this task requires a mix of specialists due to the different categories of emails that are being received in Copperline's email.

## Reflection

Email 1 reading: Matches. Diane says the other 160 pumps "will follow 15 days later," and the what_changed line in the latest saved pipeline run says 40 arrive on day 4 and the other 160 arrive on day 19, which is 15 days later. The brief gave day 19 for the 160.

Email 1 customer names: No. The reply turned down Diane's request. It says Copperline is "not able to share customer names or order details" and gives only totals instead, 3 orders affected, 55 units short.

Email 4 orchestrator: None. Both saved orchestrator runs for Email 4 made one orchestrator call and stopped there. They drafted nothing, and the latest run notes that the supply analyst never checked the data. With the same email, the pipeline ran the supply analyst (3 calls), the supplier writer and the reviewer. It found no orders at risk and no decisions for a person, and saved one short reply to Owen confirming PO-5540 is on schedule. The reviewer approved it with no revisions, and nothing was sent.
