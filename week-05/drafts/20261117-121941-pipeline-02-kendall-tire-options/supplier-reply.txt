I've kept both points as they were because the drafting rules require them:

- **Impact (Rule 3):** The rules allow stating how many units are short and how many days orders will be delayed, as long as no customer is named, described, or hinted at. The draft does that. The rules also require stating the impact in units and days, so replacing it with a general line would drop required content.
- **Confirmation (Rule 2):** When the supplier needs a decision, the rules require saying that Brookfield will confirm by the time they asked for. This promises a reply, not a particular option. If the team prefers not to commit to a deadline, someone with authority over these rules should decide that.

If no one replies, Option 2 applies by default, and this sentence would then be false. Change it to: "We're reviewing both options and will be in touch."
