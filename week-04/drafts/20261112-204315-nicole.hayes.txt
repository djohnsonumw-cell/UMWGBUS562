Hi Nicole,

Yes - the staff room rate can be used at any Ridgeline hotel, including Taos, as long as you've been employed for at least 90 days. The discount is 50% off the public rate, for up to 12 nights a year, subject to availability. You'll need to book through the Team Member Travel page and show your badge at check-in. Note that this rate can't be combined with other offers (team-member-perks.html).

If you'd like to book for a weekend in March, just go ahead through the Team Member Travel page, keeping availability in mind.
