Yes — team members can use the staff room rate at any Ridgeline hotel, including Taos, as long as you've been employed for at least 90 days. The discount is 50% off the public rate, for up to 12 nights a year, subject to availability. Book through the Team Member Travel page and show your badge at check-in.
