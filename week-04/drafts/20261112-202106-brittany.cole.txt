Keep in mind that if you leave Ridgeline before earning those days back, their value will be deducted from your final paycheck. (Source: pto-policy.html, "Borrowing PTO you haven't earned yet")

2. **Submit your request early**: Since you're requesting 5 or more consecutive days, your supervisor's approval is needed at least 30 days ahead of the start date. Since your honeymoon is about two months away, you're well within that window — just be sure to submit the request (and the borrowing request, if needed) through your hotel's scheduling system soon. (Source: pto-policy.html, "Requesting PTO")

3. **Check for blackout dates**: If any of your requested days fall on a blackout date at Ridgeline Taos, you'll also need your General Manager's sign-off. (Source: pto-policy.html, "Blackout dates")
