
## What I Built

For my prompt lab, I tested four prompts utilizing different techniques. The prompts were to help with decision making for Wildframe Coffee Roasters on a few projects to improve the company's sells and customer service. 

## Why This Approach

Task 1 - Technique: zero-shot because the company just needed ideas a simple format was already provided. With ideas, it is best for the prompt to allow for open ideas. Any other technique could have affected the ai ability to provide different ideas (thinking outside the box).

Task 2 - Technique: one-shot because the company needed 3 new cards to be made but it required a specific format. An example was provided with the specific format, nothing else was needed or else it would have complicated it.

Task 3 - Technique: few-shot because there was already one example "Wildframe's voice" and I utilized the past comments and replies as the second example to get the desired results. 

Task 4 - Technique: chain-of-thought because the company needed a recommendation based off the provided data. The data needed to be calculated to assist with an overall decision which included multiple steps.

## Reflection

All of the prompts came out the way I wanted them to with the techniques I used. Oddly enough, the techniques went in order with the tasks. I was sure to not complicate anything and use what I believed was appropriate therefore I do not have a choice that I would say I am least sure of. I think the technique that made the biggest difference is the chain-of-thought technique because the task in particular required more steps and calculations to recommend a simple location. I believe if someone is manually doing the calculation, depending on their ability to do math, they could produce different results if errors are made. While ai can make errors to, I believe it is easier to have ai to complete the step and we as the human review the step and step that is provided to ensure it is correct.
